Chapter 7: Rock Tunnel and Lavender Town

With the S.S. Anne behind you, the road goes through Rock Tunnel to Lavender Town. Team Rocket is after Prof. Hale’s research in the tunnel, and in Lavender it is after something in the Pokémon Tower.

Main quest in this chapter: rescue Prof. Hale in Rock Tunnel, climb Pokémon Tower (twice, with a trip back to Cubone in between), and reach the end of the tower story, when Agatha breaks up Team Rocket there. Afterwards the guard at the Route 8 gatehouse lets you into Saffron City.

Three things close as you go: the Underground Path (the Rock Tunnel rescue), the tower’s optional Trainers if you skip them (they vanish after the two illusion grunts), and Cynthia’s optional battle on Route 12 (she leaves when you first reach Saffron).

Rock Tunnel. Enter from the north door on Route 10 (a Pokémon that knows Flash lights the cave). Somewhere inside, Prof. Hale shouts that someone is stealing his research: Jessie, James and Meowth have him cornered. Beat them in a Double Battle. The rescue closes the Underground Path doorways (an invisible barrier stops you at each one).

Opens up:
  • Rock Tunnel: hide-and-seek (find four hidden kids)
Warning: doing this closes these for good. Finish them first.
  • Underground Path: the underground battle (story, Cascade Badge) Missable

Rock Tunnel: hide-and-seek (find four hidden kids)

Section titled “Rock Tunnel: hide-and-seek (find four hidden kids)”

Rock Tunnel Side quest

Rock Tunnel's lower level. Do not lose to the corner kid: his battle freezes the game after a loss (hack bug), so save first.

Where: the schoolkid “seeker” stands on the east side of the lower level of Rock Tunnel. The four friends are hidden across the tunnel.

How it works: you don’t need to talk to the seeker first. The friends can be found in any order, except that the fake archaeologist needs the corner kid first.

Friend Where How to expose them
Corner kid the far south-east corner of the lower level Talk to him. He battles you.
Fake archaeologist the middle of the lower level Only after the corner kid: “Believe the schoolkid?” → No, then battle.
“Pikachu” the north part of the lower level “Try to catch it?” → Yes. Then answer Yes to both questions (perfect disguise? Pikachu cutest?) to expose him with no battle; any No means a battle.
Ninja “boulder” a rock in the far north-west of the upper level “Use Rock Smash?” → Yes, then battle. You don’t need a Pokémon that knows Rock Smash.

Reward: back at the seeker: TM80 Rock Slide (once).

Warning: don’t lose to the corner kid. Save before you talk to him. His battle has no white-out: because of a hack bug, after a loss the game carries on with the “Pikachu” kid’s dialogue before the field has come back, and freezes on a black screen (tested in an emulator).

Technical source (script files and flags)

file 129 (scripts 8, 10, 11, 12, 18; L2775, L2805, L3752, L3782, L4328, L4368). Seeker ≈47,77; corner kid ≈43,98; archaeologist ≈30,86; “Pikachu” ≈22,69; boulder ≈14,10. Corner kid is trainer 606 (TrainerBattle 606 0 0 0 at L2879, third argument 0 = a loss is not allowed); a loss jumps to the “Pikachu” kid’s dialogue at L3782 (looks like a wrong jump label; hack findings D-1395, D-1548); his battle is 194. Emulator (emu_harness.py guide0107 --case corner_kid): after a real loss the field never returns (black screen, CPU running in heap memory, msg 46 never printed). Controls with the same trainer: TrainerBattle 606 0 1 0 (allowed loss, as Giovanni’s L4818) returns to Rock Tunnel; with WhiteOut after it the player wakes in the Pokémon Center.

Route 10 / Lavender Town: the girl who waits (the Lavender curse)

Section titled “Route 10 / Lavender Town: the girl who waits (the Lavender curse)”

Route 10 Lavender Town Side quest

Where: a girl on Route 10, a few steps south-east of Rock Tunnel’s south exit. The fortune-teller’s house is on Lavender’s south side; the storyteller lives next door, five steps to the west.

How it works:

  1. Talk to the girl on Route 10: “So you’ve finally come? … Please don’t go…” She vanishes. A man in the House of Memories mentions a beauty on Route 10 who vanished.
  2. In the fortune-teller’s house, ask for a reading (Yes). Having met the girl, you’re told you face “a bloody calamity” and offered 10 Cleanse Tags for $30,000. If you’re short, she takes all your money instead and still hands over the tags. Both Yes and No start the curse.
  3. The curse: every time you try to walk out of Lavender (north, west or south exit), you hear “I want to see you…” and “A mysterious force pulled you closer!”. You can’t leave on foot. Fly works.
  4. To end it, buy the tags. At an exit you’re asked “Use 10 Cleanse Tags for protection?” → Yes. You’re still pulled back, but the fortune-teller is now replaced by the girl. She appears even if you no longer have all 10 tags: the game just says “You don’t have that many Cleanse Tags!”.
  5. Go into the fortune-teller’s house and talk to the girl (just entering doesn’t start the scene). The girl turns out to be a Gastly (Lavender’s husband). “Stay in Lavender Town?”
    • Yes: the curse stays. Talk to her again to replay the scene.
    • No: battle “Gastly” (Venusaur, Zangoose, Blastoise, Lv. 36). The ghost of Lavender herself calls it off, and the curse is lifted. Losing is fine: you’re healed and Lavender still calls it off.
  6. Afterwards Gastly stays in the house and reads your fortune (badge-based flavour lines).

Reward: the storyteller next door has a new tale about Lavender and her Gastly husband. When he asks “Have you heard how Lavender Town got its name?”, answer No (Yes ends the talk), hear the legend, then answer Yes to his next question for a Heavy Ball. The House of Memories tutor takes a Heavy Ball for Night Slash.

Notes: if you never ask for a reading after meeting the girl, nothing happens. Buying the tags uses up the fortune-teller: afterwards she only asks “Have you tried my charms?”.

Technical source (script files and flags)

file 194 (script 2; the girl’s hide flag 1999), file 771 (scripts 1–3; L1025, L772, L1122, L1201; the reading sets var 0x40BA to 0 on both answers; buying sets flag 2003; the Gastly battle TrainerBattle 184 0 1 0 has no win check and lifts the curse with 0x40BA = 1), file 765 (script 10; L459, L547), file 770 (L73; storyteller script 1: Heavy Ball item 495, flag 1298, needs the curse beaten, flag 2002 clear), file 129 L1189 (Rock Tunnel sets 0x40BA = 1), file 772 script 1. Girl ≈1418,206; fortune-teller door ≈1419,249 (zone 437, file 771; in this hack it’s the fortune-teller’s house, not the Name Rater’s); storyteller door ≈1414,249.

Lavender Town: trade Big Pearls for Heart Scales

Section titled “Lavender Town: trade Big Pearls for Heart Scales”

Lavender Town Side quest

Where: the House of Memories in Lavender Town.

How it works: trade one Big Pearl for one Heart Scale. You can repeat this exchange; it is not a one-time reward.

Useful route: after earning the Super Rod, the Fishing Guru’s apprentice on Route 12 trades Net Balls for Big Pearls. Bring those pearls here, then spend the Heart Scales at Saffron’s move relearner.

Pokémon Tower: side battles and extras

Section titled “Pokémon Tower: side battles and extras”

Lavender Town Main quest

The tower is part of the main story (Team Rocket, Maxie and Mr. Fuji). You need to visit twice, with a return to Cubone in between. This sequence is the same for all starters.

How to open the way:

  1. First climb: beat Blue at the stairs, then keep climbing. The illusion Trainers, including “Mom”, are not there yet. You can optionally battle the lonely ghost girl along the way.
  2. At the upper staircase, a voice tells you to go away and forces you back. There is no choice that lets you continue here yet.
  3. Return to Mr. Fuji’s Volunteer Pokémon House (the door about eight steps south of the Pokémon Center door) and talk to Cubone. If you visited earlier, it was hostile; now it sniffs you, warms up to you and cries, then runs out of the house. Let the scene finish, including the request to find it.
  4. Return climb: go back into Pokémon Tower. The tower’s Trainers and illusions now appear, including “Mom”. Battle your way up to continue the story.

These two fights are required along the way; they do not both happen on the first climb:

  • Blue at the stairs: he blocks the whole corridor a few steps west of where you come up from the ground floor. “Challenge Blue?” Yes → Wartortle Lv. 30, Kadabra Lv. 28, Pidgeotto Lv. 29, Umbreon Lv. 28, Rhyhorn Lv. 28, Exeggutor Lv. 29. There’s no item. He stays there until you beat him, and you need that win much later: the scene in the east room of Team Rocket HQ B3F in Mahogany Town, where Ariana and Lawrence corner Crystal and the second password comes from, only plays after it (Team Rocket HQ B3F).
  • “Mom”, on the return climb after Cubone runs out, is an illusion, and she stands on the only way on from the stairs on her floor. “Do as Mom says and go home?” Yes just walks you back and she stays there. You must answer No and win: battle “Mom” (Mr. Mime, Poliwrath, Raichu, Lv. 29–30). The battle also requires the Rock Tunnel scene with Prof. Hale.

These parts are optional:

  • The Magcargo behind the illusions: in the far west corner of the floor where you fight the two illusion grunts. After you beat the two illusion grunts, “Magcargo can no longer create illusions. Catch it?” → wild Magcargo, Lv. 40. Catch it before you take any stairs: knocking it out, catching it or running away all make it disappear, and every staircase reloads the map, which also hides it. Don’t lose: a loss freezes the game (black screen), so heal and save first (tested in an emulator; see Known issues).
  • Silph Scope: after the grunt double battle, a Silph Scope appears as an item two steps north of the Magcargo.
  • The lonely ghost girl (along the north wall of one of the tower floors): “You can see me? … Play with the little girl for a while?” → Yes: battle School Kid Coco (Raticate, Mightyena, Glameow, Lv. 29) for a Spell Tag. After that she teaches Curse free, as often as you like.

Battle the tower’s Trainers as you go up on the return climb. Once you’ve beaten the two illusion grunts, every tower Trainer you skipped (Medium Colette on Blue’s floor, and the illusion “Prof. Oak”, “Archie” and “Petrel”) is gone the next time a floor loads, for example after a staircase. None of them has an item, so you only miss the battles. Tested in an emulator. The ghost girl stays.

Section titled “Lavender Town: Mr. Fuji’s gifts (after the tower)”

Lavender Town Side quest

Opens after Agatha breaks up Team Rocket in the tower. Do first: Pokémon Tower: side battles and extras.

Where: the Volunteer Pokémon House (the door about eight steps south of the Pokémon Center door), after Agatha breaks up Team Rocket in the tower.

  • Cubone: it has grown fond of you. Mr. Fuji asks whether you’ll take it. Yes gives Cubone, Lv. 5 (you need room in your party).

Route 8: Persian and Skitty (the quarrelling owners)

Section titled “Route 8: Persian and Skitty (the quarrelling owners)”

Route 8 Side quest

Where: Route 8, east of the Saffron gatehouse. A man with a Persian stands on the north side of the road about 11 steps east of the gatehouse door; a woman with a Skitty (she calls it Glameow at times) stands much further east along the same north edge, toward Lavender.

Who gets it / when: anyone, once you can reach Route 8 east of the gatehouse. The owners are there from the start.

How it works:

  1. Talk to the man with the Persian first (he praises mechanical Poké Balls). This is required: until you do, the woman only talks about Apricorn Balls.
  2. Talk to the woman with the Skitty. Her Skitty runs off south, and both owners move to the meadow on the south side of the route, roughly in the middle.
  3. Walk into the meadow (from the west or from the north-east). The owners argue while their Pokémon refuse to fight each other. Your character steps in.
  4. Double Battle against both owners: Guitarist Jon (Persian Lv. 29, Swellow, Girafarig) and Aroma Lady Bella (Skitty Lv. 28, Chansey, Mothim). Losing whites you out; the scene replays.
  5. Afterwards, talk to the man (now in the meadow).

Reward: TM40 Aerial Ace (one time).

Notes: before the Rock Tunnel rescue of Prof. Hale, a second pair of owners is already standing in the meadow, with the meadow scene armed. The rescue removes that pair and disarms the meadow; talking to the woman after the man arms it again.

Route 12: the boyfriend who lost his Pokémon (you must lose)

Section titled “Route 12: the boyfriend who lost his Pokémon (you must lose)”

Route 12 Side quest

Where: the north part of Route 12, a couple standing a few steps north-east of the fisherman who complains about the fish-zapper.

How it works:

  1. Talk to the girlfriend first. Team Rocket killed her boyfriend’s Pokémon and she asks you to battle him.
  2. Talk to Ace Trainer Newt. He refuses, and a menu opens:
Option Result
“Don’t you want revenge?” he’s still afraid, try again
“Man up and snap out of it!” fails
“You’re a useless coward!” fails
“You’ve got a girlfriend!” he accepts a battle

  1. He has a single Rattata, Lv. 13. Lose on purpose (this battle doesn’t white you out). If you win, he wails “I’m worthless!” and you have to try again. If he wins, he gets his confidence back.

    Watch out: his Rattata knows only Normal-type moves (Tail Whip, Focus Energy, Quick Attack, Tackle), so a team of only Ghost types can’t lose this battle. And a Pokémon with no PP left never uses Struggle, so once your moves run out the battle can’t end at all and you have to reset (known issue). Bring a Pokémon that isn’t a Ghost type.

  2. Talk to the girlfriend: Zoom Lens.
