Chapter 12: The Alto Mare Islands, Viridian Gym and the Earth Badge

Sabrina has sent you to the Alto Mare Islands. The reason is simple: the Viridian Gym stays closed until you pass Granny Mae’s trial on Two Island, and the Earth Badge is the last Kanto Badge you need for the Victory Road gate.

Main quest in this chapter: take the ship from Vermilion to the Alto Mare Islands, find a partner for Granny Mae’s trial on Two Island (it turns out to be Blue) and pass her five-stage test, then go back to Viridian City and win the Earth Badge from Giovanni.

The Alto Mare travel agency, which sails to Four, Five, Six and Seven Island, is closed for “high-tide season” until Johto’s sea route reopens, and Three Island’s biker business starts then too. You do not have to wait for the ferries, though: One Island and Two Island are open, and with Surf you can sail to every island yourself (see the optional step on Four to Seven Island). The Island Pilgrimage, the Island Cave story and the biker business come much later.

The Earth Badge also ends the Pidgeot loan for Pikachu starters.

Two Island Main quest

Only while Granny Mae's trial is waiting for a partner.

Notes: if you’re stuck at “this trial needs a team”, this is the person to talk to.

Two Island: Granny Mae’s ultimate-move trial

Section titled “Two Island: Granny Mae’s ultimate-move trial”

Two Island Main quest

Opponents are Lv. 72-76. Do first: Two Island: a partner for Granny Mae's trial.

Where: Granny Mae’s training hall on Two Island, behind the north-east door of the town (the northernmost door). The receptionist stands a few steps inside the entrance.

Who gets it / when: from your first visit to the islands (after the Saffron incident). Required for the story: the Viridian Gym stays closed until you pass this trial. The trial only starts with two challengers. Talk to the receptionist alone first: she tells you to find a partner. Then invite the Johto Trainer on Two Island; Blue turns up instead and waits at the reception (Two Island: a partner for Granny Mae’s trial).

How it works: talk to the receptionist with Blue there. There’s no quitting halfway; the guards send you back if you try.

  1. Singles assessment: Veteran Logan (Flygon 72, Typhlosion 72 …).
  2. Knowledge quiz: walk up to the quiz guide. Five questions. You answer all five before she tells you whether you passed. If you got any wrong, walk back up to her to take the quiz again (no penalty).
# Question Answer
1 Highest expected power, counting accuracy? Fire Blast
2 Most accurate move with no weather? Leaf Storm
3 Most likely to trigger its added effect? Fake Out
4 Which is NOT a signature move? Sacred Fire
5 Which item does NOT boost a move’s power? Dragon Scale
  1. Doubles assessment: Ace Trainer Charles (Abomasnow, Jynx, Glalie, Mamoswine, all Lv. 73), a Double Battle.
  2. Aptitude check: a single battle against Blue (Pidgeot 75, Rhydon 74, Alakazam 74, Blastoise 76).
  3. Granny Mae’s test: a Multi Battle with Blue at your side against Granny Mae (Skarmory, Kingdra, Altaria, Dragonite, Lv. 75) and Aroma Lady Dana (Vileplume, Tentacruel, Rotom, Azumarill, Lv. 74). Stages 3, 4 and 5 run straight into each other as one scene: once you step up to Charles, you don’t get control back until the Multi Battle is over, so nothing heals you between these three battles (only items used during a battle). Heal before you walk up to Charles. Your party is healed after the Multi Battle.

A loss at any stage whites you out. Talk to the receptionist to try again; she restarts the whole trial from stage 1. Reward:

  • Granny Mae teaches the ultimate moves free, as often as you like: Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn or Hydro Cannon, but only to the final forms of every region’s starters (Venusaur, Meganium, Sceptile, Torterra, Serperior, Chesnaught, Decidueye; Charizard, Typhlosion, Blaziken, Infernape, Emboar, Delphox, Incineroar; Blastoise, Feraligatr, Swampert, Empoleon, Samurott, Greninja, Primarina). A Pokémon that already knows one of the three can’t learn another.
  • Talk to Aroma Lady Dana, who stands next to Granny Mae, afterwards: Ninja Scroll (“unlocks Greninja’s potential”), once.
Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 782 (scripts 5–10, 14; L1305–L5449, L2545–L8994; vars 0x40b0, 0x40ab; flags 1105, 1705, 2915; stage 3 is coord script 9, and after TrainerBattle 643 (Charles, L2323) the same script goes on through L4379 to TrainerBattle 644 (Blue, L4615) and MultiBattle 645/646/647 (L5001) with no HealParty and no ReleaseAll in between; heal at L5164; a loss jumps to L4309, the restart; the Ninja Scroll (item 761, L2648) comes from object 4, the examiner Granny Mae calls “Dana” in the scene; the Viridian Gym door guard checks flag 1705, file 741 script 2). Hall entrance: Two Island door ≈116,107 → hall 47–48,126; receptionist ≈48,122; Dana ≈37,39.

Section titled “Two Island: Fern’s “sick” Chatot (Chatot needed)”

Two Island Side quest

Your first healthy Pokémon must be a Chatot.

Where: Pokémon Breeder Fern’s clinic on Two Island: the house just east of the man watching the Butterfree (the southernmost house door in town). Fern is right inside, with her admirer Mr. Summers and a Chatot that keeps squawking “My head hurts!”.

Who gets it / when: any time. The first Pokémon in your party that isn’t fainted must be a Chatot.

How it works: talk to Fern’s Chatot and answer “Have your Chatot try talking to this Chatot?” → Yes. Pick what your Chatot says:

Phrase Result
Hello! / Have you eaten? / My tummy hurts flavour; Fern keeps treating it. Try again.
I’m faking the Chatot repeats it, Fern realises Summers taught it to fake the illness, and he runs off.

Reward: 2 Moomoo Milk + 2 Sitrus Berries. One time.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 782 (scripts 1–3, L1256, L3147–L7333; flag 1637; species check 441 = Chatot). Clinic: Two Island door ≈104,121; Fern ≈5,101 inside.

One Island: the composer’s title → Miracle Seed → TM86 Grass Knot

Section titled “One Island: the composer’s title → Miracle Seed → TM86 Grass Knot”

One Island Side quest

Where: the composer at the back of the One Island Pokémon Center (straight north from the entrance), and the woman with a Roserade outside, a few steps north of the fountain (about 9 steps north and 6 steps east of the Pokémon Center door).

How it works:

  1. The composer wants a title for his piece about the islands. Answer “Mysterious Girl” (“City of Water” and “Garden of the Sea” are rejected; retry freely). He gives you a Miracle Seed.
  2. Bring a Miracle Seed to the Roserade woman: say Yes to learning her technique, then trade the seed. She gives you TM86 Grass Knot. Reward: Miracle Seed, then TM86 Grass Knot (the only TM86 in the game).

Notes: the woman may never offer the trade. Her “already given” marker is shared with Misty’s Gyarados gift and with the early end of the Viridian Gym trials. If either of those has happened, she treats you as already served and only explains how Grass Knot works (see Known issues).

Technical source (script files and flags)

script files 889 (script 4, L361–L413; flag 1115), 845 (script 6, L1205–L1324; flag 1081, also set by Misty’s Gyarados gift and the early Viridian Gym end). Composer ≈8,7 in the Pokémon Center; Roserade woman ≈148,138 (Pokémon Center door ≈142,147).

One Island: the Island Restaurant’s battle meals (repeatable)

Section titled “One Island: the Island Restaurant’s battle meals (repeatable)”

One Island Side quest

The diners' teams are Lv. 65.

Where: the Island Restaurant on One Island: the door just west of the Pokémon Center on the south row. The two waiters stand on the right-hand side of the dining room: the upper one serves the snack set, the lower one the fruit set.

How it works: order a meal. While you wait, three diners challenge you in a row; a loss whites you out and the money is gone.

Meal Price Battles (Lv. 65 teams) Reward
Specialty snack set $2,000 three Single Battles: Fisherman Arno, Pokéfan Monte, Psychic Hans Lava Cookie + Old Gateau
Flavorful fruit set $3,000 three Double Battles: Sailor Thor, Ace Trainer Wynn, Hiker Dobbs Lum Berry + Jaboca Berry + Rowap Berry

Notes: repeatable as often as you can pay. The snack set is an easy source of the Lava Cookies and Old Gateaux that the Five Island monk’s Snorlax wants. The Treecko owner in a nearby house (the north-west door of the town) teaches Grass Whistle for a Rare Candy (tutor list).

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 894 (scripts 1, 2; L877–L1658). Restaurant door ≈134,147, waiters ≈11,36 and ≈12,39 inside; the Treecko owner is behind door ≈134,127 (same script file, different building).

Alto Mare Waters: Charmander’s owner and the lifeguard

Section titled “Alto Mare Waters: Charmander’s owner and the lifeguard”

Alto Mare Waters Side quest

Needs Surf.

Where: a middle-aged man by a sign in the western part of the Alto Mare Waters, and the lifeguard with a Lapras and a Vulpix in the waters east of Three Island.

How it works:

  1. The man says a lifeguard got his Charmander killed and asks you to kill the lifeguard’s Lapras. Answer “I’ll see justice done”.
  2. Find the lifeguard and talk to him from his left (west) side; from anywhere else you only see him gazing at the shore. Choose “Charmander’s death involves you?”, and hear his story: in a high tide he saved the wild Vulpix, and the Charmander drowned. Choose:
Choice What happens
“Lapras’s life for Charmander’s!” you battle the lifeguard (Swimmer♂ Benson, Lapras 62 lead). The owner arrives, thanks you and gives the reward, then tries to hurt Lapras; Vulpix calms him down
“I guess it isn’t your fault” the owner arrives furious and you battle him (Pokéfan Jasper, Raichu 62 lead); Vulpix calms him down

Reward: HP Up either way, and the owner makes peace with the lifeguard.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 965 (scripts 1, 4; L999, L2431–L4908; flags 2201, 2202; the lifeguard’s talk only proceeds when the player faces right, GetPlayerFacing = 3 at L971). Positions: owner ≈82,122 (sign ≈82,119); lifeguard ≈213,125.

Alto Mare Waters → islands → back: the Manaphy Egg

Section titled “Alto Mare Waters → islands → back: the Manaphy Egg”

Alto Mare Waters Side quest

Needs Surf. The girl in the Alto Mare Waters starts it; you follow her to Six, Five and Four Island and back. The treasure hunters are Lv. 62-65.

Where: a girl with an Egg in the eastern part of the Alto Mare Waters, about 10 steps north-west of the lifeguard.

How it works:

  1. She asks how to hatch an Egg. Answer “Walk around with the Egg”. She sets off.
  2. Follow her trail. Four Island: the Mystery Stone buyer (don’t sell it)

Section titled “Four Island: the Mystery Stone buyer (don’t sell it)”

Four Island Side quest Missable

Needs Surf and Mr. Fuji's Mystery Stone. Do not sell it. Closes with the Rising Badge. Do first: Lavender Town: Mr. Fuji's gifts (after the tower).

Where: a stone collector at the far south end of Four Island (well south of the ferry man and the nurse), and a woman with a Misdreavus in the middle of town (east of the man with the Graveler).

Who gets it / when: once you carry Mr. Fuji’s Mystery Stone (Lavender Town: Mr. Fuji’s gifts) and before the Rising Badge.

How it works:

  1. He offers $100,000 for the Mystery Stone. Selling is a trap: you need the stone for the Charizardite (Dragon Shrine, or Cerulean Cave for Charmander starters).

Notes: after the Rising Badge he no longer buys the stone, so you can’t sell it then.

Five Island: the four-leaf clover (speak up for him)

Section titled “Five Island: the four-leaf clover (speak up for him)”

Five Island Side quest Missable

Needs Surf. Only while a sick Trainer is on Five Island: do it before you meet the Virtue examiner in the Viridian Gym.

Where: a clover hunter by a tree on the east side of the island (south-west of the east-path monk), and a Trainer with a sick Pokémon, about 12 steps east of the south-entrance monk.

Who gets it / when: while the sick Trainer is there. If you’ve already met the examiner of the Viridian Gym Virtue trial (he asks you to help someone in Viridian City), the sick Trainer is gone and the hunter only says “a four-leaf clover should be growing somewhere around here!”. To start it then, go into the house by the One Island ferry (south-west of Five Island). If a sick Trainer is standing in front of the old monk with a Xatu at the back on the left, talk to the monk: the sick Trainer asks him about the four-leaf clover, and after that the scene by the tree can play.

How it works: talk to the hunter. He finds a four-leaf clover and wants $2,000,000 for it; the other man only has $300,000.

  1. “Speak up for him?” → Yes. The hunter tells you to pay the other $1,700,000 yourself or leave.
  2. “What will you do?” → “You’re selling it today”: you invoke your Champion powers. Beat Guitarist Zenith (Dugtrio 62, Raticate 63, Swellow 64, Luxray 65) and he sells it for $300,000.
  3. Answering No to “Speak up for him?”, or choosing “Forget it”, ends the scene: the sick man goes off to search elsewhere. Reward: none for you; the sick Pokémon gets its medicine.

Notes: one chance: the scene doesn’t repeat. If there’s no sick Trainer in the ferry house either, the clover scene can’t be started for now. That visitor comes and goes with some Johto story scenes.

Island Forest (Six Island): before the Lucky Meowth God

Section titled “Island Forest (Six Island): before the Lucky Meowth God”

Island Forest Side quest Missable

Do these before the Lucky Meowth God scene in the post-game, which changes the Island Forest for good.

The Island Forest changes for good only if you let Jessie, James and Meowth stay on Six Island and then walk into the forest (Six Island: Deoxys and the Lucky Meowth God). Do these first: after that, the people below stay where they are, but they switch to other lines and their quests can no longer be done. A Spoils you haven’t returned stays in your Bag. The statue attendant moves next to the statue and stops charging.

The forest has two entrances from Six Island: a wide one on the west side and one on the east side.

  • The toll thug (Roughneck George, in the north-east with his Ursaring and Zangoose) demands $3,000 to let you through. Choose “Pay and go through”, or “Force your way through” and beat him (Ursaring, Zangoose, Tauros, Drapion, all Lv. 63). Paying only gets you through that once; after a win, the path is free from then on.
  • The stolen doll: Hiker Brown (about 9 steps east of the west entrance) wants a battle (Golem 62, Donphan 63, Vigoroth 64, Honchkrow 65). Win to get the Spoils (a key item). Give it to the crying girl about 9 steps south of the west entrance (“Give it back”): TM20 Safeguard.
  • The wishing pond (a girl in the middle of the forest, about 10 steps east of a Trainer with a Flygon): give her an Amulet Coin (once). She throws it in and asks for your wish:
Wish Effect
I want to get rich overnight flavour only

I want to get stronger flavour only
I don’t really have a wish flavour only
  • The lucky statue (an attendant with a Shiftry in the south-east of the forest): $500 to view it (flavour). When the Lucky Meowth God arrives he moves beside the statue and stops charging.

Viridian City: Yellow’s first Pokémon (the Gym’s Virtue trial)

Section titled “Viridian City: Yellow’s first Pokémon (the Gym’s Virtue trial)”

Viridian City Main quest

Do first: Two Island: Granny Mae's ultimate-move trial.

Where: Yellow, the girl standing with her uncle and his Doduo just south of the Viridian Mart.

Who gets it / when: anyone, once the Viridian Gym has reopened. Until then the man at the Gym door says Giovanni is away; the Gym reopens after Granny Mae’s ultimate-move trial on Two Island, when Blue says he’s heading to the Viridian Gym (Two Island: Granny Mae’s ultimate-move trial). Then talk to the man just inside the Gym door again, from directly in front of him (coming from the door): he is now the Virtue examiner. From the side he only says your virtue qualifies you, and nothing happens. Before that, Yellow only chats about her uncle’s Doduo.

How it works:

  1. The examiner says you must help someone in Viridian City.
  2. Talk to Yellow and answer Yes to “This little girl wants a Pokémon. Help her out?”.
  3. You need a plain Poké Ball in your Bag. It is taken and handed to Yellow. Without one, your character goes off to buy one; come back and ask again.
  4. You’re taken to Route 2 and battle a Lv. 5 Rattata (a scripted battle, so you have to win). Yellow throws the ball and catches it. If you lose, you white out and the ball is gone: bring another Poké Ball and ask Yellow again.
  5. Back in Viridian, Yellow gives you her thanks gift.

Reward: 1 Big Pearl, and the examiner now lets you through (“That’s proof enough of your virtue”).

Notes: This is the Gym’s first trial. The Wisdom quiz and the Skill trainers come next (next entry). Yellow’s story continues later (Viridian Forest rescue, romance route).

Viridian Gym: the Wisdom quiz (two ways to pass)

Section titled “Viridian Gym: the Wisdom quiz (two ways to pass)”

Viridian City Main quest

Do first: Viridian City: Yellow's first Pokémon (the Gym's Virtue trial).

Where: the second examiner inside the Viridian Gym, after the Virtue trial.

How it works: five questions. Any wrong answer and he still asks the rest, then turns you away at the end without saying which one was wrong. You can retry at once. There are two valid answer sets. The honest one:

# Question Answer Position
1 Why do you want to be a Pokémon Trainer? It’s my childhood dream 2nd
2 What do you think catching Pokémon depends on? Mutual trust 4th
3 As a Pokémon Trainer, what’s the key to beating your opponent? Bonds with Pokémon 3rd
4 How do you see Pokémon? Partners in battle or Family I live with 1st or 2nd
5 What matters most in holding together an organization as big as the Pokémon League? Shared ideals 4th

The Team Rocket set (“You ignored the empty slogans and told the truth… worthy of meeting Lord Giovanni!”): To make money (4th), Force and conquest (2nd), Powerful Pokémon (1st), Tools for my goals (3rd), Big cash rewards (3rd). Reward: passage to the Skill trial and Giovanni. Both answer sets count as a pass.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 741 (script 6, L1293/L3339, flag 1631).
