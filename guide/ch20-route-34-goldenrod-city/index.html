Chapter 20: Route 34, Goldenrod City and the Plain Badge

Goldenrod City is the biggest town so far, with a Department Store, an underground shopping street, a Game Corner, a Radio Tower and a Gym. Whitney is not in her Gym when you arrive: she is at the Radio Tower, where a radio show called “The Strongest Trainer” is about to record its first guest. How you answer the director decides how you earn the Plain Badge.

Main quest in this chapter: reach Goldenrod City through Route 34, record the show on Radio Tower 4F (let Whitney do it, or do it yourself) and win the Plain Badge at the Goldenrod Gym.

Before the Plain Badge: Prof. Rowan’s Chimchar (he leaves with the Gym win) and the underground battlers. Before Silver’s scene on Route 37 (Chapter 21 onwards): Goh and the Lopunny owner, the Purugly girl’s “act” and Mary’s secret photos. Before the Glacier Badge: the Game Corner magnet cheater. The love reading at the fortune-teller must be done before the League HQ’s round-3 order (see the romance entry).

Route 34. North of Ilex Forest’s gatehouse the Day-Care stands by the road with a police officer and his Arcanine a few steps north-west of its door; a Youngster with a Sunkern in the north-west is still smarting from Whitney (he only helps you after the Plain Badge, and only until the Rising Badge). A dancer with a twisted ankle north of the gatehouse tells you about the dance master in a hidden grove at the very south end of the route (bring a Potion). The north end of Route 34, close to Goldenrod, is where the Salamence scene will play later.

Route 34: Totodile, the dance master (one chance)

Section titled “Route 34: Totodile, the dance master (one chance)”

Route 34 Side quest Missable

Needs a Potion for the dancer, then a free party slot twice. Only 'Strike a battle-command pose' works; every other dance ends it. The two dancers are Lv. 62-65.

Where: a dancer with a twisted ankle on the path between the Day-Care and the Ilex Forest gatehouse, about 25 steps north of the gatehouse. The dance-off grove is at the very south end of Route 34, below the Ilex Forest gatehouse, reached by following the water south.

Who gets it / when: after the Ilex Forest chapter (the dancer isn’t there before).

How it works:

  1. Have a Potion and talk to the dancer → “Give him your Potion?” → Yes. He tells you about the dance master in the hidden grove. The Potion isn’t taken.
  2. Walk into the grove. Two dancers try their moves on Totodile and fail. “Go have a dance-off with Totodile?” (No: step back onto the tile to be asked again.)
  3. “What kind of dance will you perform?” Only “Strike a battle-command pose” works. “Copy Totodile’s dance”, “A daring moonwalk” and “Give up” end the quest for good.
  4. Totodile likes you → “Take Totodile as your partner?” → Yes. The two dancers step in: beat them in a 1-vs-2 Double Battle (Super Nerd Null and Aroma Lady Lydia, Lv. 62–65).
  5. Talk to Totodile again → Yes.

Reward: Totodile Lv. 5.

Notes: losing the Double Battle blacks you out. You can retry. You need a free party slot in steps 4 and 5. If you say No in step 4 or 5, just talk to Totodile again later.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 237 (scripts 9, 14, 15; L3258, L5047, L3288–L5922, L6830–L7071; var 0x408b 11 → 12 → 13; coord script 14 on 0x408b = 11; trainers 766+767; flag 1839). Positions: dancer ≈371,425; grove ≈370,472.

Route 34: advice for Whitney’s victim (→ Razor Claw)

Section titled “Route 34: advice for Whitney’s victim (→ Razor Claw)”

Route 34 Side quest Missable

The reward needs the Plain Badge and must be claimed before you win the Rising Badge.

Where: a Youngster with a Sunkern in the north-west of Route 34, north-west of the police officer and his Arcanine.

Who gets it / when: after the Ilex Forest chapter. The reward needs the Plain Badge, and you must claim it before you earn the Rising Badge. After that he only boasts about finally beating Whitney.

How it works: he’s still smarting from Whitney’s Miltank. Choose “Here’s how to beat Miltank” or “Keep trying, you’ll win” (“Not my problem” does nothing). Without the Plain Badge he waves the advice away, and you can come back. With it, he’s impressed.

Reward: Razor Claw (once).

Notes: on the normal path (bug hunt finished and reported to Bugsy) this always works. It can only be lost in an edge case: if you left Ilex Forest in the middle of the bug hunt and earned the Rising Badge before ever reporting to Bugsy.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 237 (script 16; L3696–L7248; flag 2102; CheckBadge 10 and 15). Flag 2102 is also set during the bug hunt and cleared when it ends. Youngster ≈356,397.

Goldenrod Magnet Train Station: Prof. Rowan’s Chimchar (missable; before the Plain Badge)

Section titled “Goldenrod Magnet Train Station: Prof. Rowan’s Chimchar (missable; before the Plain Badge)”

Goldenrod City Side quest Missable

Pick the Chimchar up before you win the Plain Badge.

Where: Magnet Train Station 1F: Prof. Rowan and his assistant stand in the middle of the hall, up and to the right of the entrance. The station door is just east of the Radio Tower.

Who gets it / when: from your arrival in Goldenrod until you win the Plain Badge. The Gym win removes Rowan and his assistant for good.

How it works:

  1. Talk to Prof. Rowan. He recognises you and offers a Sinnoh Pokémon: “Accept Prof. Rowan’s Chimchar?”
  2. Yes: you get Chimchar Lv. 5 (nickname prompt). You need a free party slot; with six Pokémon he says to come back.
  3. No: nothing changes, so you can talk to him again and accept later, as long as he’s still there.

Reward: Chimchar Lv. 5.

Notes: once you have the Plain Badge he’s gone, so pick it up before the Gym.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 890 (Magnet Train Station 1F, script 10, L2062–L2181; objects 4/5 hidden by flag 1613, set in file 883 L577). Positions: Rowan ≈11,9 in the station; station door ≈348,349.

Goldenrod City: Goh and the Lopunny owner (win with Normal types only)

Section titled “Goldenrod City: Goh and the Lopunny owner (win with Normal types only)”

Goldenrod City Side quest Missable

Closes with the Route 37 scene with Silver (a later chapter). Goh's team is Lv. 84-85.

Where: the west plaza at the far west edge of town (the old Wi-Fi center site). A man stands there with his Lopunny; Goh is a few steps south-east of him.

Who gets it / when: after the Ilex Forest chapter. The scene is closed by the Route 37 scene with Silver.

How it works:

  1. Talk to the Lopunny owner. Goh mocks Normal types, and his Blaziken knocks out the man’s Lopunny with Close Combat. “Go teach Goh a lesson?”
  2. No: Goh mocks you as a coward and leaves. The event is over, with no reward.
  3. Yes: you confront him and he dares you: “beat me with Normal-type Pokémon!” The game checks your party against a long list of non-Normal species. Pure Normal types and Normal dual types such as Pidgeot or Fearow pass (Raticate is pure Normal in this hack). The list has quirks: it refuses Braviary but lets Staraptor and Stunfisk through.
    • Any other Pokémon in the party: “go swap in Normal-type Pokémon”. Goh stays next to the plaza. Talk to him again with an all-Normal party: “Beat Goh using Normal-type Pokémon?”
    • Battle One-Ball Man Goh: Machamp 84, Rhyperior 85, Magnezone 84, Hariyama 85, Blaziken 84, Bastiodon 85 (a Fighting/Rock/Steel team built to beat Normal types).
  4. Win: Goh storms off (“I need the power of legendary Pokémon”). Lose: you white out and can retry.

Reward: none besides the scene.

Notes: Goh’s team is at a post-game level at this point in the story.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 882 (scripts 2 and 5, L2105–L2872, L15488–L16230; trainer 769; flags 792/1614). The owner is male (573#16); “No” gets the coward line (573#21). Positions: Lopunny owner ≈314,350; Goh ≈322,353.

Goldenrod City: the Purugly girl and Team Rocket’s “act” (choice with two endings)

Section titled “Goldenrod City: the Purugly girl and Team Rocket’s “act” (choice with two endings)”

Goldenrod City Side quest Missable

Finish the Ilex Forest bug hunt first. Closes with the Route 37 scene with Silver (a later chapter).

Where: two small houses that share one interior:

  • the clinic in the north-east of town, beside the Flower Shop, where Jessie, James and Meowth are recovering;
  • the Purugly girl’s house on the east side of town, a little north-east of the Department Store (a little girl is crying a few steps west of its door).

Who gets it / when: from your arrival in Goldenrod until the Route 37 scene with Silver. That scene decides the outcome for good. Finish the Ilex Forest bug hunt first (see Notes).

How it works:

  1. Clinic: Jessie, James and Meowth are patients after the Ilex Forest fight. You choose between two options:
    • “You’re under arrest!”: they flee. They’re back next time you enter, and you can choose again.
    • “I’ll let you off today”: they promise to behave. This is the choice you need.
  2. Purugly girl: she wants to release her Purugly because it got “ugly” after evolving. Choose “Surely you love it by now?” and talk it through. She refuses to change her mind, but this unlocks the next step.
  3. Clinic again, with Team Rocket spared: talk to them, choose “Ask Team Rocket for help”, and they agree to play the villains in her house.
  4. Walk into the girl’s house: Team Rocket bursts in and does their motto. You step in. Jessie battles you first; James only battles you if you beat Jessie. Losing doesn’t white you out.
What you do Result
Lose on purpose (the plan; losing to Jessie is enough) Purugly jumps in and chases Team Rocket off. The girl realises how much Purugly loves her and keeps it.
Win Team Rocket complains that “this isn’t what we agreed on” and leaves. The girl gives you a Nugget, but she still releases Purugly later.

Reward: a Nugget if you win. If you lose, you get no item, but she keeps Purugly.

Notes:

  • If you never finish the act, or you win it, she has released Purugly by the time you pass Route 37 (“I released my Purugly into the wild… I miss it so much now”). If you lost the staged fight, she says she likes Purugly’s round body more every day.
  • This quest shares its progress with the bug hunt’s Ariados. On the normal path (hunt finished) it starts clean. If you left Ilex Forest in the middle of the hunt, the act can start as soon as you enter the girl’s house, the clinic choice can be skipped, or the game can count it as “Purugly kept”. Finishing the hunt after starting this quest wipes your Purugly progress. (Suspected hack bug.)
Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 897 (the shared clinic/house interior: scripts 1–8, L2154–L7251; var 0x409d 2→6; flags 2158/2160; trainers 13/23), outcome in file 246 (Route 37: L387 always sets flag 2252; L391–L404 also set flag 2159 unless 0x409d = 5; file 897 L963 checks 2159 (released) before 2252). Var 0x409d is also the bug hunt’s Ariados state. Doors: clinic ≈376,335; girl’s house ≈373,362.

Goldenrod City: the popped Air Balloon (→ Fashion Case)

Section titled “Goldenrod City: the popped Air Balloon (→ Fashion Case)”

Goldenrod City Side quest

Needs an Air Balloon (Department Store 2F).

Where: a little girl crying a few steps west of the Purugly girl’s house door, on the east side of town.

How it works: she bought a balloon at the Department Store and it popped. If you carry an Air Balloon, you’re asked “Give the little girl an Air Balloon?” Yes: she takes one and gives you a Fashion Case.

Reward: Fashion Case (one time; once you have it she only daydreams about floating like a Drifloon).

Notes: Air Balloons are sold on Department Store 2F ($300 each, 3 for $900, 5 for $1,500, 10 for $3,000). Whitney also gives 10 if you earn the Plain Badge the friendly way (see the Gym entry).

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 882 (script 33, L15963–L16013), file 896 (script 24). Girl ≈370,362.

Goldenrod Dept. Store 6F: the blind man’s Poochyena (one chance)

Section titled “Goldenrod Dept. Store 6F: the blind man’s Poochyena (one chance)”

Goldenrod City Side quest Missable

Suneo and Reina are Lv. 62-65.

Where: Department Store 6F (rooftop atrium), at the far west (left) side, an old man with a Poochyena.

How it works: two shoppers bully a blind old man into leaving because his guide dog is a Poochyena. You get one choice: “Speak up for the old man?”

  • Yes: you argue and they challenge you to a Double Battle: Guitarist Suneo (Rhydon 62, Noctowl 63, Relicanth 64, Staraptor 65) and Beauty Reina (Chansey 62, Furret 63, Luvdisc 64, Torterra 65). Win: the old man gives you his White Flute. Lose: you white out and can try again.
  • No: he leaves with his Poochyena for good, and the shoppers gossip about the “vicious dog”.

Reward: White Flute.

Notes: the game doesn’t check your party size before this battle. From rc8, with fewer than two Pokémon the battle becomes a Single Battle. On rc7 and earlier, bring at least two Pokémon: with only one, a broken second Pokémon appears beside yours and the game freezes when you choose FIGHT (tested in an emulator; known issue).

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 901 (script 11, L2210–L3303; flags 2146/2147; trainers 777+778). Old man ≈2,6 on 6F.

Radio Tower → Goldenrod Tunnel: Mary’s secret photos (→ Cherish Ball; missable)

Section titled “Radio Tower → Goldenrod Tunnel: Mary’s secret photos (→ Cherish Ball; missable)”

Radio Tower Goldenrod Tunnel Side quest Missable

Closes with the Route 37 scene with Silver (a later chapter): report back to Mary before it. Chaz is Lv. 62-65; the photographer Moose is Lv. 70.

Where: Radio Tower 4F (Mary), a clerk in the street a few steps south of the Radio Tower door, then the underground warehouse.

Who gets it / when: from your arrival in Goldenrod until the Route 37 scene with Silver. That scene closes the case and cancels the reward.

How it works:

  1. Talk to Mary on Radio Tower 4F. She feels someone is secretly photographing her and asks you to look into it.
  2. Talk to the clerk south of the Radio Tower. He adores Mary and once found a pile of her photos in the Underground warehouse. Once Mary has asked you, a menu appears: choose “About Mary’s photos…”. He gives you the Basement Key.
  3. Go down into the underground shopping street (Goldenrod Tunnel B1F). In its south-east corner a “NO ENTRY BEYOND THIS POINT” sign blocks a door. With the Basement Key you can go through. Behind it is a small room with stairs down to B2F.
  4. On B2F, a guard stands in front of the warehouse door: beat Shady Guy Chaz (Seadra 62, Glalie 63, Metang 64, Weavile 65).
  5. Inside, you walk in on a photographer selling photos to Cassidy and Butch. Beat him: Roughneck Moose (Kangaskhan, Magmar, Sceptile, Skuntank, all Lv. 70). The Rockets escape. He admits he took the pictures for Team Rocket. Losing to either Trainer whites you out.
  6. Go back to Mary: she thanks you and gives you a Cherish Ball.

Reward: Cherish Ball.

Romance route: how it’s unlocked, how your partner is chosen, and what locks a partner out (central entry)

Section titled “Romance route: how it’s unlocked, how your partner is chosen, and what locks a partner out (central entry)”

Side quest Missable

A mixed entry. The love reading in Goldenrod must be done before the League HQ's round-3 order (after the Silver Conference); the Dream World choice, the confessions in the final chapter and the dates come much later.

Origin HeartGold has a hidden romance route. You pick one partner and confess in the final chapter. The scripts then offer post-game dates for some partners only, and moving in appears unreachable for everyone (table in step 4). Four steps decide it. Steps 1 and 2 are missable: the love reading must come before the MooMoo Farm investigation, which the League HQ’s round-3 order opens after the Silver Conference.

1. The love reading (Goldenrod fortune-teller; before the MooMoo Farm investigation)

Section titled “1. The love reading (Goldenrod fortune-teller; before the MooMoo Farm investigation)”

After the ideal-Pokémon reading (entry above), talk to the fortune-teller again and choose “My love fortune” ($10,000; she refuses if you have less). She asks up to five questions. Only one set of answers works:

Question Answer that works
“Would you be happy to have a companion who secretly adores you?” I’d be glad to be liked (“Just friends is better” ends the reading here)
“Could you accept a romance with more than two people?” No, I’m loyal to one
“What kind of person are you hoping fate brings you together with?” The opposite sex
“At what age do you think it’s OK to start dating?” After 10
“What do you think matters most in a relationship?” Shared memories or Mutual attraction
  • All correct: “Your fated love is surely coming in the near future.” The romance is fully open.
  • Any other answer: “…may lie somewhere in the distant future”, or with “Just friends is better”, “friendship will be the one and only theme of your adventures.” You still pay. There is no retry, and in practice every partner ends up locked out at step 2 (see Notes).

Partner check (optional, repeatable, $10,000 each): after a “near future” result, “My love fortune” lets you name a friend. Male players: Green, Misty, Yellow, Cynthia, Crystal. Female players: Blue, Red, Steven, Gold, Silver. “The two of you share a special bond” means that partner is still open. “The bond between you two has run its course” means they’re locked out. Use it to check your earlier choices (table below).

The fortune-teller stops offering “My love fortune” once you’ve fought Will and Karen in the Dream World (step 2). Do the reading before the MooMoo Farm investigation.

2. The Dream World: choose your partner (MooMoo Farm, Route 39; League HQ round 3)

Section titled “2. The Dream World: choose your partner (MooMoo Farm, Route 39; League HQ round 3)”

The MooMoo Farm investigation (sick Miltank, Route 39; MooMoo Farm: the sick Miltank investigation) only opens with the League HQ’s round-3 order (League HQ), after the Silver Conference. That order makes the farm’s Miltank sick. Until then they’re healthy and the farmer only talks about his milk, so you can’t get to the Dream World earlier. The investigation is one of the round’s leads, so everyone goes through it. It ends with Will and Karen opening a rift in the stable, and you’re pulled into the Dream World.

  1. You arrive at the south end. Walk north and talk to the old man first. He stands on the path north, next to a signpost. He’s only there if your love reading said “near future”.
  2. “Do you like dreaming, young one?” → Yes. (“No” ends the talk and he never asks again. He only ever asks once.)
  3. “So, who do you usually see in your dreams?” Pick your partner. Male players: Green, Misty, Yellow, Cynthia, Crystal. Female players: Blue, Red, Steven, Gold, Silver. “No one” picks nobody.
  4. Everyone you didn’t pick is locked out for good.

Then walk west or east to the story scene: Will catches Cresselia (west) and Karen catches Darkrai (east). They team up against you in a Double Battle (Rocket Elites Will and Karen, six Pokémon each, Lv. 90). Losing whites you out. They leave you stranded, and the old man disappears after this battle. To get out, examine the broken statue at the north end of the Dream World. It gives you a Lunar Wing if you don’t have one. Examine it again with the Lunar Wing to return to the MooMoo Farm stable.

When you leave through the statue without having named a partner (no love reading, a “distant future” result, answering No, choosing “No one”, or doing the battle before you talk to him), every partner is locked out.

Radio Tower Goldenrod City Main quest

Where: Radio Tower 4F, then the Goldenrod Gym on the north side of town.

Who gets it / when: story. Whitney is at the Radio Tower when you arrive. She (and Gold) only appear in the Gym after the recording scene.

How it works: in the studio the director drops Whitney as the first guest of “The Strongest Trainer” in favour of you. You choose:

Choice What happens at the Gym
“Let Whitney do it” Whitney is won over (she still insists you record the show). At the Gym she battles you straight away: single battle, Leader Whitney. Win: Plain Badge, 10 Air Balloons and her phone number.
“I’ll do the show” Whitney storms off, furious. At the Gym she calls you “the Champion who loves picking on people”. Say Yes to “want the Plain Badge?” and she demands a 2-on-2 Multi Battle: you with the Quilava kid (Gold, waiting in the Gym) against Whitney and Norman.

The Multi Battle route, step by step:

  1. Talk to Gold in the Gym: “Team up to challenge Whitney”. He’s in.
  2. Go to Norman’s house (the Friendship Checker house), about a dozen steps west of the Pokémon Center door. His friend Prof. Birch is visiting, and Norman’s son Ruby has taken Birch’s daughter Sapphire out. If you haven’t been inside before, the scene asks him for you automatically. If you had already visited the house, choose “Invite Norman to the Gym”. Norman agrees, but first the kids must be found. Earlier you can see Ruby promising to show Sapphire “the tall grass” on Department Store 1F.
  3. Find Ruby and Sapphire. They’re in trouble on Route 34 (see the Route 34 entry).
  4. Back at the house, Norman punishes Ruby. If you had already asked him to come to the Gym, he goes straight there. Otherwise he sees Birch off and waits at the Magnet Train Station: talk to him there. He asks how to raise Ruby: “Teach him, don’t hit him” or “Spare the rod, spoil the kid” (this only changes Ruby’s later lines). Then choose “Whitney asked me to invite you”.
  5. With Norman and Gold both in the Gym, talk to Whitney. Stand in front of her: she refuses to talk to you from behind. Multi Battle (partner Gold vs Whitney + Norman). Win: Plain Badge and her phone number, but no Air Balloons. Losing whites you out, and you can retry.

Reward: the recording itself gives 3 Max Elixirs and 2 Heart Scales whichever you choose. The interview’s first part changes with your starter, but only the dialogue. At the Gym you get the Plain Badge either way, and the friendly route adds 10 Air Balloons.

Notes: after the badge, the man standing just right of the Gym door teaches Hyper Voice for a Love Ball, and Whitney offers Singles/Doubles rematches (Doubles needs two Pokémon). After the Hall of Fame, Norman’s house also has Steven (singles, doubles, or a Double Battle against Steven and Norman together).

Route 34: Ruby, Sapphire and the wild Salamence (part of the Goldenrod Gym chain)

Section titled “Route 34: Ruby, Sapphire and the wild Salamence (part of the Goldenrod Gym chain)”

Route 34 Main quest Missable

Only on the Multi Battle route (you chose 'I'll do the show'). The wild Salamence is Lv. 60.

Where: the north end of Route 34, near Goldenrod City.

Who gets it / when: during the Goldenrod Gym story, after Norman asks you to find his son Ruby (Goldenrod Friendship Checker house). The scene plays when you walk into the north end of Route 34.

How it works: Ruby is picking a fight with an angry Salamence. You step in: wild Salamence, Lv. 60. You can catch it, knock it out or run. Then Ruby and Sapphire go home.

Reward: a Lv. 60 Salamence if you catch it. One chance: it’s gone afterwards, whatever happened.

Notes: losing blacks you out and the scene plays again.

Technical source (script files and flags)
