Chapter 23: Routes 38 and 39, Olivine City and the Mineral Badge

Route 38 and Route 39 lead from Ecruteak City to Olivine City, a harbour town with a Gym, a Café and a Lighthouse. At the south end of Route 39 Gold and Crystal stop you for the scene that opens the sea routes: from then on the ferries run and several errands on the Sevii Islands wake up. In Olivine you win the Mineral Badge, and Team Rocket takes over the Lighthouse straight afterwards.

Main quest in this chapter: cross Routes 38 and 39, battle Gold at the south end of Route 39, win the Mineral Badge from Jasmine and drive Team Rocket out of the Lighthouse. Amphy, the Lighthouse Pokémon, is poisoned: the medicine comes from Cianwood City (next chapter) and the reward waits at the top of the Lighthouse.

Before the Mineral Badge: the guard’s letter to Momo (Chapter 15) and giving Crystal your Super Rod. Before the Rising Badge (Chapter 27): the Three Island bikers’ errands.

Route 38. West of Ecruteak the road is short: a kid with a Murkrow that stole a coin (“Nugget trap”) hides behind the fence at the east end, a Magician trains Apricorns in the west part and a merchant sells TM78 Captivate at the west end. Route 38 to Olivine City also has a handful of small extras (Route 39’s Hiker, the Café’s drunk veteran, the Aron kid’s Endeavor lesson).

Opens up:
  • Route 38: the Murkrow that stole a coin (Nugget trap; → 2 Rare Candies, 2 Lava Cookies or keep the Amulet Coin)

Route 38: the Murkrow that stole a coin (Nugget trap; → 2 Rare Candies, 2 Lava Cookies or keep the Amulet Coin)

Section titled “Route 38: the Murkrow that stole a coin (Nugget trap; → 2 Rare Candies, 2 Lava Cookies or keep the Amulet Coin)”

Route 38 Side quest

Where: the east end of Route 38, about 20 steps west of the Ecruteak gatehouse. The kid hides behind the fence; the Murkrow sits 7 steps south of him.

Who gets it / when: any time. One time only.

How it works:

  1. Talk to the kid and choose “OK, I’ll get it back”. (“Figure it out yourself” just ends the talk; you can ask again.) Until you agree, the Murkrow only cries at you.
  2. Talk to the Murkrow → “Ask Murkrow for the coin?” → Yes.
    • No Nugget in your Bag: it eyes you warily and hops away to another spot on the route. Follow it and try again.
    • With a Nugget: “Trade the Nugget to Murkrow for the gold coin?” Say No. If you say Yes it takes your Nugget and then hops away to another spot, just as when you have none. The Nugget is lost; follow the Murkrow and try again with another Nugget.
    • After refusing the trade: “Beat up Murkrow and take the gold coin by force?” → Yes → a wild Murkrow, Lv. 25. Beat it (losing whites you out) and it drops the coin: an Amulet Coin. Catching it or running away counts as a win too: the coin still drops. So you need a Nugget in your Bag to get the fight at all, but you must refuse to hand it over.
  3. Go back to the kid → “Did you get my coin back?” → Yes to hand over the Amulet Coin, or No to keep it.

Reward: hand the coin back → 2 Rare Candies + 2 Lava Cookies (the Amulet Coin is taken). Or keep the Amulet Coin and get nothing else. He’ll take it any time later if you change your mind.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 247 (scripts 5, 6; L580–L1105, L1107–L1748; flags 2160, 1614). Yes to the trade: L1976 takes the Nugget (item 92) at L1983, then L1467 (same hop-away as without one). Murkrow battle WildBattle 198 L1681, CheckBattleWon L1688 false only on a loss (→ L1568), so a flee drops the coin like a win. Kid at ≈332,173, Murkrow at ≈332,180.

Route 38 → Olivine City: small extras

Section titled “Route 38 → Olivine City: small extras”

Route 38 Olivine City Side quest

  • Route 38, Magician Lott (west part of the route): an Apricorn-picking Trainer offers a Singles or Doubles battle any time. His team is Lv. 81–82 (Wobbuffet, Smeargle, Ditto, Porygon-Z, Aerodactyl, Meganium). Losing whites you out. Repeatable, no prize.
  • Route 38 merchant (at the west end of the route) sells Burn Heal $250, Ice Heal $250, Grass Mail $500 and TM78 Captivate for $3,000.
  • Route 39, Hiker Kenneth (just south-east of the Sunflora old lady): the same kind of optional Singles or Doubles battle, Lv. 81–82 (Kabutops, Corsola, Armaldo, Rhyperior, Skarmory, Dugtrio). Losing whites you out.
  • Olivine Café, the drunk veteran (in the middle of the Café): one surprise battle (Veteran Gilbert: Qwilfish Lv. 63, Magneton Lv. 64, Swellow Lv. 65, Rampardos Lv. 65). Losing whites you out. No prize.
  • Olivine City, the Aron kid (a few steps west of the Gym door) teaches Endeavor for a Heavy Ball (tutor list).
  • Olivine City salesman (in the south-west of town, about 11 steps south of the Café door): sells Weakness Policies (1 for $500 up to 20 for $10,000).
  • Photo spots: after your final Hall of Fame entry, Lt. Surge poses for a photo at MooMoo Farm (Fridays and Saturdays), Sabrina at Olivine Harbor (Fridays) and Jasmine in the Olivine Café (with her phone number registered). The Route 39 photographer is there only on Thursdays and Sundays.

Route 39: the “Team Rocket grunt” who wants $10,000 (optional Lv. 62–65 battle)

Section titled “Route 39: the “Team Rocket grunt” who wants $10,000 (optional Lv. 62–65 battle)”

Route 39 Side quest

Where: Route 39, by the MooMoo Farm stable, a few steps east of the right-hand stable door.

Who gets it / when: from your first visit. One time.

How it works: he demands $10,000 “to stay safe”.

  • Yes (pay): he’s stunned and doesn’t take your money at all. No battle, no cost. He stays for another try.
  • No: a tough battle (Lickilicky Lv. 62, Arbok Lv. 63, Weezing Lv. 64, Victreebel Lv. 65). Losing whites you out. Win, and he admits he’s a fan in a fake uniform. Choose “Arrest him first” (he begs; it just asks again) or “Just give him a talking-to” to end it.

Reward: none besides the Exp. Afterwards he stays as a reformed kid.

Technical source (script files and flags)

script file 249 (script 12, L527–L868, L1938, L2220–L2390; trainer 807; flag 1620). Grunt at ≈274,166.

Three Island: the Alto Mare Bikers (save the Meowth?)

Section titled “Three Island: the Alto Mare Bikers (save the Meowth?)”

Three Island Side quest Missable

A man in the One Island Pokémon Center asks you to drive off the bikers. The bikers leave Three Island for good when you win the Rising Badge.

Where: Three Island. Two bikers block the north entrance. More bikers stand outside the gang’s house (the Islander’s House on the north side, a biker guarding its door), and one biker is picking on a stray Meowth a few steps north-west of them.

Who gets it / when: once a man in the One Island Pokémon Center has asked you to drive off the bikers and you’ve agreed. He only turns up after Gold’s battle on Route 39 in Johto (on the way to Olivine City). Until you’ve agreed, the guards just turn you away. Missable: once you clear the Dragon’s Den (Clair, Rising Badge), the bikers leave Three Island for good.

How it works:

  1. Talk to the entrance guards: double battle (Bikers Ivan and Warren, Lv. 62–64). They run to tell their Boss.
  2. Pick one of two ways to bring out the Boss:
    • The house bikers: double battle → the Boss wakes up → another double battle → the Boss himself (Black Belt Doyle, Breloom Lv. 72 lead).
    • The Meowth: the prompt is “Save this Meowth?”. Say Yes and beat the cat catcher. The Boss comes out and orders his men to grab the Meowth.
  3. On the Meowth route, a talking Meowth leaps in to protect the stray, “Meowzie”, and Jessie & James join the fight. Then you’re asked “Help Team Rocket?”:
    • Yes: you battle the Boss (Black Belt Doyle) yourself, then Team Rocket finish the gang. Losing whites you out.
    • No: you only watch; Team Rocket beat the bikers by themselves. Either way, Meowth then duels Meowzie’s Persian “big brother” for her and wins. Meowzie turns him down anyway (“a Meowth that talks like a human is just too creepy!”), and Team Rocket leave.
  4. The bikers retreat to their Chief at the Shipyard Ruins (continued in Shipyard Ruins: the bikers’ Chief). A biker at the top of the bridge south of the town won’t let you through until you’ve talked to Big Sis, in the southern of the two houses, right next to him.

Reward: no item for the fight itself. The story ends at the Shipyard Ruins: winning the gauntlet there earns a Protector (missable: before you clear the Dragon’s Den); taking the Chief’s bribe costs you a $400,000 fine later.

Notes: saying “No” to “Save this Meowth?” does nothing; you can talk to him again. The “Help Team Rocket?” choice only decides whether you fight the Boss.

Shipyard Ruins (Three Island): the bikers’ Chief, a bribe or a fight

Section titled “Shipyard Ruins (Three Island): the bikers’ Chief, a bribe or a fight”

Shipyard Ruins Side quest Missable

Only during the biker occupation, until the Rising Badge. The Protector you win is for the Donphan owner on Route 39. The Chief's gauntlet is Lv. 62-64.

Where: the Shipyard Ruins, the door at the south-west corner of Three Island. Biker Muramasa stands in front of the door: beat him to get in (Kangaskhan, Scizor, Shiftry; Lv. 62–64; a loss whites you out). Inside, the biker at the back of the ruins (top right, surrounded by four bikers) asks if you’re here to see the Chief.

Who gets it / when: during the Three Island biker occupation, after you’ve talked to Big Sis (until then the biker at the top of the bridge south of her house won’t let you come this way). Missable: once you finish the Dragon’s Den (Clair, Rising Badge), the bikers are gone for good and Three Island becomes a tourist spot anyway.

How it works: the Chief is Roughneck Paxton, the old Kanto Speed Alliance leader from Cycling Road. Whatever you answer to “Do you remember me?”, he offers you a red envelope.

  • Accept the red envelope: you get $200,000 and leave. The bikers stay. The next time you walk down the path south of the One Island Pokémon Center (towards the harbour, next to the tourist who asks if it’s your first time in the islands), a League discipline inspector fines you $400,000, double the bribe (all your money if you have less). After that you can come back and fight, but Big Sis is no longer on your side (see the gauntlet’s last step).
  • Refuse: a gauntlet, with no healing in between (a loss whites you out):
    1. Bikers Yancy + Hugh (Double Battle, Lv. 62–64).
    2. Bikers Lucky + Bert (Double Battle).
    3. Biker Muramasa again (same team as at the door).
    4. If you taught Big Sis to ride and didn’t take the bribe, she switches sides and you face Paxton + Black Belt Doyle together. Otherwise you face Doyle + Big Sis (Double Battle), then Paxton alone (Tentacruel, Lunatone, Snorlax, Cacturne, Rhyperior, Umbreon; Lv. 81–82). The gang gives up Three Island and leaves. Reward: no item from the Chief. Win the gauntlet and a man who wants to rebuild the island appears on Three Island, a few steps west of the northern house door (the same man who asked you, in the One Island Pokémon Center, to drive the bikers off). He gives you a Protector. Missable: he’s only there between the gauntlet and the Dragon’s Den clear, which hides him again. Players who only take the bribe never get it. The tourist spot and the Three Island pilgrimage trial come from finishing the Dragon’s Den, whether or not you fought.

Notes: you can take the bribe again after the fine, but each time you’re fined again at One Island ($400,000) and must beat Muramasa at the door again, so it always loses money. Taking the bribe even once cancels Big Sis’s help: after the fine the game treats her as never having been taught, and she has already left her house, so you can’t teach her again.

Island Cave (Four Island): Crystal Onix and Granny Lisa

Section titled “Island Cave (Four Island): Crystal Onix and Granny Lisa”

Island Cave Side quest

Four Island: talk to the craftsman first. One chance at the Crystal Onix; save and bring Poké Balls. The Crystal Onix is Lv. 40.

Where: the Stone Workshop on Four Island (the craftsman, Islander’s House) and a hidden part of the Island Cave, reached through the passage in its north-west corner.

Who gets it / when: any time after you reach Four Island. Talk to the craftsman first; until then the man at the passage blocks the way (tested in an emulator).

How it works:

  1. Talk to the stone craftsman in the Stone Workshop. He’s hunting for the legendary Crystal Onix.
  2. In the Island Cave, go to the north-west corner. Until you’ve talked to the craftsman, a man stands in front of the passage there (“There’s nothing in the cave up ahead”) and you can’t get past; afterwards he’s gone. Go through it: you come out in a small area, and the old woman is about ten steps west of where you arrive. Walk up to her. Yes (keep the secret) she gives you a Star Piece. Talk to her again: she entrusts Onix to you and leaves. Then talk to the Onix: wild Crystal Onix, Lv. 40.
No she throws you out. Talk to the craftsman in the Stone Workshop: he hurries off to the cave. Talk to him in the hidden area where you met her: he finds only chips and gives you a Shiny Stone. No Onix.

Reward: the chance to catch Crystal Onix (Onix, form 1) on the secret route, or a Shiny Stone on the other.

Notes: one chance. The Onix disappears as soon as the battle starts, so if you knock it out, run or white out, it’s gone. Save first and bring Poké Balls. The choice can’t be undone.

Olivine City: Sitrus Berries → Machine Part → Diving Suit → HM05 Whirlpool (with a Tentacool)

Section titled “Olivine City: Sitrus Berries → Machine Part → Diving Suit → HM05 Whirlpool (with a Tentacool)”

Olivine City Side quest

Needs 20 Sitrus Berries, then a Tentacool in your party for the boy. The Lighthouse engineer is away between the Mineral Badge and the end of the Rocket fight.

A chain across four places in Olivine. HM05 Whirlpool is the reward. Crossing whirlpools (for example to the Whirl Islands, League HQ round 4) needs the Volcano Badge. According to the scripts a Pokémon that knows Whirlpool isn’t required (Field moves: which Badge each one needs), but that isn’t confirmed in game, so teaching it is the safe choice.

How it works:

  1. Olivine Café: the man with two Machoke, just left of the entrance, wants to feed them Sitrus Berries. Carry 20 Sitrus Berries and say Yes → he takes 20 and gives you a Machine Part. With fewer than 20 he declines. (The man with the Pelipper in Olivine, a few steps south of the Lighthouse door, sells Sitrus Berries: 1 for $1,000, 3 for $3,000, 5 for $5,000, 10 for $10,000.)
  2. Olivine Lighthouse 1F: the engineer, a few steps up and to the right of the entrance, needs a machine part for the elevator. Talk to him with the Machine Part → Yes → Diving Suit. He isn’t there from the moment you win the Mineral Badge until you’ve driven Team Rocket out of the Lighthouse; after that he’s back at his spot (Amphy doesn’t need to be cured). Tested in an emulator.
  3. Olivine City, north-east house (the left-hand of the two doors side by side in the north-east of town): a boy is afraid of the sea since a Remoraid knocked him into it. He needs a Water-type to get used to and a Diving Suit. Talk to him with a Tentacool in your party and the Diving Suit → Yes → pick the Tentacool. It must not be your only Pokémon; picking an Egg or another species just gets a comment.

Reward: HM05 Whirlpool. Your Tentacool and the Diving Suit are taken (the Tentacool then lives in his room).

Olivine Gym: Jasmine’s rule (three Pokémon with base Defense 130+)

Section titled “Olivine Gym: Jasmine’s rule (three Pokémon with base Defense 130+)”

Olivine City Main quest

Carry three or fewer Pokémon, each on Jasmine's Defense list. Jasmine's team is Lv. 80.

Where: Olivine Gym. Jasmine is at the back.

Who gets it / when: after Gold’s battle at the south end of Route 39 (he’s just won the Mineral Badge). This is the Mineral Badge.

How it works: Jasmine’s rule is a 3-on-3 Single Battle using only Pokémon with a base Defense of 130 or higher.

  • Carry 3 Pokémon or fewer. With 4 or more she refuses.
  • Every Pokémon must be on her list. The game checks a fixed list of species meant to match base Defense 130+, for example Steelix, Skarmory, Forretress, Shuckle, Aggron, Metagross, Bastiodon and Probopass. Aegislash is refused, though Doublade is allowed, and a few weak species slip through (Stunfisk, Yungoos and Gumshoos; see Known issues). Eggs are skipped by the check.
  • Full list of obtainable species that pass: Golem, Cloyster, Onix, Steelix, Forretress, Shuckle, Skarmory, Nosepass, Probopass, Lairon, Aggron, Torkoal, Dusclops, Dusknoir, Relicanth, Metagross, Regirock, Registeel, Bastiodon, Rhyperior, Leafeon, Uxie, Lugia, Groudon, Gigalith, Cofagrigus, Carracosta, Ferrothorn, Doublade, Carbink, Avalugg, Toxapex, Pyukumuku.
  • Jasmine: Steelix, Metagross, Skarmory, all Lv. 80. Losing whites you out, and you can retry.

Reward: Mineral Badge. She starts to give you a TM, but a Gym Trainer bursts in: Team Rocket is at the Lighthouse. The TM is only handed over later (next entry).

Notes: her Gym Trainer by the entrance teaches Metal Burst (tutor list), but he’s gone from the moment you win the badge until you’ve cured Amphy (next entry). Jasmine can be rematched later (Singles or Doubles).
