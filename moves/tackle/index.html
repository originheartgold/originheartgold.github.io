Tackle

Effect

A physical attack in which the user charges and slams into the foe with its whole body.

Move stats
TypeCategoryPowerAccuracyPPPriorityTarget
NormalPhysical40100350Selected target

Changes in Origin HeartGold

Compared with Pokémon HeartGold.

  • Power: 35 → 40.
  • Accuracy: 95% → 100%.

509 Pokémon and forms can learn this move. Check availability before planning your team.
