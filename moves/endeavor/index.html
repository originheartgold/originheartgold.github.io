Endeavor

Effect

An attack move that cuts down the foe’s HP to equal the user’s HP.

Move stats
TypeCategoryPowerAccuracyPPPriorityTarget
NormalPhysicalvar.10050Selected target

“var.” means power depends on the battle.

Changes in Origin HeartGold

No changes listed.
