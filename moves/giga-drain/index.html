Giga Drain

Effect

A nutrient-draining attack. The user’s HP is restored by half the damage taken by the target.

Move stats
TypeCategoryPowerAccuracyPPPriorityTarget
GrassSpecial75100100Selected target

Changes in Origin HeartGold

Compared with Pokémon HeartGold.

  • Power: 60 → 75.

Pokémon that learn this move

367 Pokémon and forms can learn this move. Check availability before planning your team.

TM / HM acquisition and compatibility: TM19.
