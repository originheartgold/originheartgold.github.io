Recover

Effect

A self-healing move. The user restores its own HP by up to half of its max HP.

Move stats
TypeCategoryPowerAccuracyPPPriorityTarget
NormalStatus——50User

“—” power marks a status move. “—” accuracy means no accuracy check.

Changes in Origin HeartGold

Compared with Pokémon HeartGold.

  • PP: 10 → 5.

Pokémon that learn this move

148 Pokémon and forms can learn this move. Check availability before planning your team.
