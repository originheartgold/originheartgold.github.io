TM95 Heavy Slam

TMs & HMs pocket · buy for $4,000, sells for $2,000

Effect

The user slams its heavy body into the foe. The heavier the user is than the foe, the stronger it is.

Follow the linked quests for story requirements. A listed source does not always mean you can get the item more than once.
