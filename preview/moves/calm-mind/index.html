Calm Mind

Effect

The user quietly focuses its mind and calms its spirit. Raises Sp. Atk and Sp. Def by 1 stage.

Move stats
TypeCategoryPowerAccuracyPPPriorityTarget
PsychicStatus——200User

“—” power marks a status move. “—” accuracy means no accuracy check.
