Goldenrod City

Johto

Quests and events

  • Goldenrod Magnet Train Station: Prof. Rowan's Chimchar (missable; before the Plain Badge)
  • Goldenrod City: Goh and the Lopunny owner (win with Normal types only)
  • Goldenrod City: the Purugly girl and Team Rocket's "act" (choice with two endings)
  • Goldenrod City: the popped Air Balloon (→ Fashion Case)
  • Goldenrod Dept. Store 6F: the blind man's Poochyena (one chance)
  • Goldenrod Underground: drive out the underground battlers (optional; before the Plain Badge)
  • Goldenrod Department Store basement: the Machop moving company (→ three items)
  • Goldenrod Game Corner: the magnet cheater (before the Glacier Badge)
  • Goldenrod City: the fortune-teller's "ideal Pokémon" reading (one per game)
  • Goldenrod City: small extras
  • Goldenrod Underground: small extras
  • Radio Tower 4F → Goldenrod Gym: the Plain Badge, two ways

Gifts, trades and scripted encounters

  • Gift: Chimchar (Lv. 5) — Goldenrod City — Magnet Train Station 1F

    Speak to Prof. Rowan in Goldenrod Magnet Train Station with a free party slot before winning the Plain Badge; that win removes him permanently.

    See Goldenrod Magnet Train Station: Prof. Rowan's Chimchar (missable; before the Plain Badge)
  • Gift: Ekans (Lv. 15) — Goldenrod Game Corner

    Goldenrod Game Corner prize for 700 coins; requires a Coin Case and a free party slot.

  • Gift: Abra (Lv. 15) — Goldenrod Game Corner

    Goldenrod Game Corner prize for 200 coins; requires a Coin Case and a free party slot.

  • Gift: Dratini (Lv. 15) — Goldenrod Game Corner

    Goldenrod Game Corner prize for 2,100 coins; requires a Coin Case and a free party slot.

  • Trade: Budew for Ditto ("Ditto")

    Trade a Budew when Goldenrod is not under Team Rocket occupation. One trade per game.

Some of these depend on your starter, story progress or a choice. See the quests above.
