Gifts, trades and scripted encounters

  • Gift: Lapras (Lv. 70) — Silph Co., staff cafeteria (hostages’ room)

    During the Saffron Rocket takeover, speak to the cafeteria hostage after Jessie, James and Maxie. Missable: collect Lapras before the Rocket Boss battle; the hostages leave afterward, win or lose. Leave a free party slot.

    See Silph Co. (Saffron takeover): the employee's Lapras (missable)
  • Gift: Hitmonlee (Lv. 35) — Saffron City — Hunyuan Dojo

    Talk to Ma Baoguo, defeat his four disciples and then Ma, and choose this Poké Ball with a free party slot. Choose only one: Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan; the other remains sealed.

    See Saffron City: the Hunyuan Dojo (beat the disciples → Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan)
  • Gift: Hitmonchan (Lv. 35) — Saffron City — Hunyuan Dojo

    Talk to Ma Baoguo, defeat his four disciples and then Ma, and choose this Poké Ball with a free party slot. Choose only one: Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan; the other remains sealed.

    See Saffron City: the Hunyuan Dojo (beat the disciples → Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan)

Some of these depend on your starter, story progress or a choice. See the quests above.
