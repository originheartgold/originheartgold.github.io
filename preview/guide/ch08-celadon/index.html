Chapter 8: Saffron City and Celadon City

Saffron City is open to you now, and its west gate leads out to Route 7 and Celadon City. Celadon is Erika’s town, and Team Rocket’s hideout lies beneath the Game Corner.

Main quest in this chapter: pass through Saffron City, fight alongside Misty on Route 7, help Erika find her stolen Gloom (the hunt sends you to the Condominiums roof and the Game Corner owner), get through the Rocket hideout (the Persian statue password is a day-and-hour puzzle) and win the Rainbow Badge. The badge lets you use Surf, but the Surf HM itself is not given here: it lies on the ground in Fuchsia Forest (next chapter).

Things that close in this chapter: Misty’s sparring match on Route 7 ends the Bicycle-lending offer in Cerulean, and the Celadon hideout finale empties the Team Rocket warehouse on Route 2.

Walkthrough

Section titled “Walkthrough”

Saffron City. The first time you walk in, Lance brings you, Blue and Green (Red for a girl) to meet the Pokémon League President in the Silph Co. building (he gives you 3 Rare Candies). Once that scene is over, the guards on Route 7 open Saffron’s west gate. Cynthia leaves Route 12 at this point, so battle her there first if you want her optional fight.

Saffron’s own quests are open now, all of them before Team Rocket takes the city over later in the game. Sabrina’s willpower test at the Saffron Gym is part of the main story: it sends Sabrina to Pokémon Tower, so you have to do it before you can go for the Marsh Badge. You can do it right now (pick up the Mind Plate in the pocket dimension before you leave it). Also here: Cynthia and Steven in the Gym and the Silph Co. lobby, the Copycat’s transformation show, the dog lover (bring a Growlithe), the Hunyuan Dojo and a few small extras.

Opens up:
  • Saffron Gym: Sabrina’s willpower test and the pocket dimension (before the takeover)
  • Saffron City: Cynthia and Steven (after the League President’s meeting)

  • Saffron City: show the dog lover a Growlithe (unlocks Fire Punch)
  • Saffron City: the Hunyuan Dojo (beat the disciples → Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan)

Route 7: Misty’s sparring match (what closes the Bicycle offer)

Section titled “Route 7: Misty’s sparring match (what closes the Bicycle offer)”

Route 7 Main quest

Not a quest, but this story scene closes the Cerulean Bicycle-lending offer: stepping onto the spot just outside the Saffron gatehouse door (two steps north-west of it) starts Misty’s battle with a Flareon trainer, then a Multi Battle beside Misty against two berserk Pokémon, Aggron and Cacturne, Lv. 45 each. Celadon Gym: sneaking in dressed as a girl (if you insulted Erika)

Section titled “Celadon Gym: sneaking in dressed as a girl (if you insulted Erika)”

Celadon City Main quest

Only if you chose ‘Perfume is pointless’ (male players).

Where: after the “pointless” answer, your friend waits a few steps south-east of the Gym door. On the first attempt this is always Misty, Charmander starters included. Charmander starters get Green instead only after a failed attempt (“Bring it on!” or losing to Erika). Talk to your friend and they dress you up in a quiet corner.

How it works (the Gym guide’s questions):

Question Answer that works Wrong answer
“Do you like perfume?” I like it “I don’t like it” just repeats the question
“The path splits here. Go straight or turn?” either, no difference —
“I have to test your strength… Is that all right?” I’d like to save my strength… “Bring it on! I’m not scared!” gives you away: you’re thrown out of the Gym and have to talk to your friend again

Then Goh exposes you anyway and you battle Erika. Losing whites you out and resets the disguise; talk to your friend again. Winning leads straight into the Gloom theft (next entry). The Rainbow Badge is not handed over yet.

Celadon City: the stolen Gloom (story chain, easy to get stuck)

Section titled “Celadon City: the stolen Gloom (story chain, easy to get stuck)”

Celadon City Main quest

Do first: Celadon Department Store 5F: Erika’s perfume question (male players).

When: right after you beat Erika in the Celadon Gym. Someone steals her Gloom, and the story won’t move on until you find out who has it. Nothing in the dialogue tells you where the thief’s auction house is, which is why so many players get stuck here.

Before you start: have $50,000 for step 5. For step 4 you also need one of these:

  • a Bulbasaur, Ivysaur, Venusaur, Charmander, Charmeleon, Charizard, Squirtle or Wartortle in your party (Blastoise doesn’t count, see Known issues). You don’t lose it;
  • or $65,535 on hand.

If you use the money route, $65,535 covers both steps: the auction boss only checks your money and doesn’t take any, so you still have enough to pay the $50,000 afterwards.

Celadon at a glance: the north row has three doors: the Department Store (west), the Condominiums (middle) and the Pokémon Center (east). The Game Corner and the Prize Corner are in the middle of town, the Restaurant is south of them, and the Gym is in the southwest corner.

Step by step:

  1. Erika waits outside, a few steps east of the Pokémon Center door. Talking to her is optional; she comments on each lead you find.
  2. Lead 1: the Restaurant. Your friend (Green for male players, Red for female players) is standing right inside the entrance. Talk to them: they heard about a secret auction house for rare Pokémon.
  3. Lead 2: the Department Store, 1F. Cynthia is a few steps up and to the right of the entrance. She saw a man in black carrying a Pokémon across the road. You need both leads before the next step. The order doesn’t matter.
  4. The auction house is on the roof of the Celadon Condominiums. No one tells you this outright (a guest in the Condominiums lobby only hints that the thief may be hiding there).
    • Enter the Condominiums (middle door of the north row) and go straight to the elevator in the top-left corner of the lobby. It says it’s “for Game Freak only”, but you can still use it. Choose Roof.
    • On the roof, a waiter with a Granbull stands in front of the small rooftop room on the right. With both leads he battles you: Waiter Grant (Granbull Lv. 36, Gyarados Lv. 37, Manectric Lv. 37). With only one lead or none, he just turns you away. Losing whites you out; come back and try again.
    • Go through the door. Talk to the boss at the back of the room and pick one of these answers:
Answer What happens
“Someone brought a Gloom to sell?” He plays dumb. Doesn’t work.
“I’m here to buy Pokémon” Works if you carry $65,535 or more (no money is taken).
“I have rare Pokémon to sell” Works if your party holds one of the Pokémon listed above.
“Sorry to bother you” Leave.

You can talk to him again as often as you like. A working answer gets the boss to brag that the Gloom was bought by “the Collector”. 5. Lead 3: the ruined gambler. He’s on the street in the middle of town, about a dozen steps east of the Prize Corner door (below the Pokémon Center).

  • If you don’t have a Coin Case, he begs for $50,000. Pay him and he gives you his Coin Case (it’s the only one in the game).
  • Talk to him again: he tells you the Collector is Lawrence, the owner of the Game Corner.
  • If you talk to him before the auction house, he only says “Have I seen Erika’s Gloom? Isn’t it in the Gym?”. That doesn’t mean he’s the wrong person; come back after step 4.
  1. Go back to Erika (east of the Pokémon Center) and tell her. The search ends, and the story moves on to the Game Corner and the Team Rocket hideout below it.

If you’re stuck, check:

  • The waiter on the roof only turns you away: you’re missing one of the two leads (step 2 or 3).
  • The auction boss throws you out: you don’t have one of the listed Pokémon in your party (the PC doesn’t count) and you’re carrying less than $65,535.
  • The gambler won’t talk about the Collector: you haven’t got a working answer from the auction boss yet, or you don’t have the Coin Case.
  • Not enough money: win trainer battles or sell items. Without the money (or one of the listed Pokémon) the story can’t continue.

script files 783 (scripts 24, 25; L2992, L3059, L3081, L3103, L3125, L4521, L4633, L6061), 787 (script 5), 803 (script 8), 794 (script 7, lobby hint), 797 (script 3, waiter), 798 (script 1, auction boss), 799 (left Condominiums elevator); gym win clears flag 1179 (file 784 L1392); the waiter and auction staff appear after the Route 7 scene (file 853 L1840 clears flag 1797). Celadon Rocket warehouse: the secret passage and the Persian statue password

Section titled “Celadon Rocket warehouse: the secret passage and the Persian statue password”

Celadon City Main quest

Do first: Celadon City: the stolen Gloom (story chain, easy to get stuck).

Where: the Team Rocket base under the Celadon Game Corner. You go in with Misty.

How it works:

  1. Launcher program: a computer on the first floor, about ten steps west of the stairs you come down from the Game Corner, runs the secret passage program; a cabinet slides away to reveal the stairs down (the Game Corner scene must have happened first).

  2. The password: a Persian statue at the far west side of a lower floor blocks the lab. The control computer, at the far east side next to where James stands, wants a five-digit password, each digit 1–6. Clues on the floor:

    • a document on a computer next to a device that keeps going “Morse… Morse…”: “Stored separately on five devices. Arrange them from bottom to top and from right to left”;
    • a device flashing six colours in order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple (= 1–6);
    • five devices blinking Morse for a colour: RED=1, ORA=2, YEL=3, GRE=4, BLU=5, PUR=6. The cryptographer in the Celadon Pokémon Center gives the Morse letters you need.
    • The devices change with the day of the week, and the last one with the hour.

    Just enter the code for the in-game weekday (on the website, the calculator below the table works it out from the day and hour):

Weekday Password
Sunday 1 6 5 4 x
Monday, Tuesday 2 4 3 1 x
Wednesday 4 3 6 5 x
Thursday 5 4 1 2 x
Friday 2 5 1 3 x
Saturday 3 4 6 5 x

x depends on the hour: 00–03 → 1, 04–07 → 2, 08–11 → 3, 12–15 → 4, 16–19 → 5, 20–23 → 6. A wrong digit isn’t flagged until the end (“Incorrect password”); retry as often as you like.

  1. Beyond the statue: the lab trainers, Jessie & James, and the Executives on the floor below.

Misty’s gifts (on the way down): after Jessie’s sneak attack wounds Misty’s Starmie, Misty gives you HM07 Waterfall. Male players who chose Bulbasaur also get her Gyarados (Lv. 20, Intimidate, all IVs 31, OT Misty). With a full party she keeps it for you; talk to her again. She lets you keep it at the end of the Celadon arc.

Celadon Department Store roof: the prize quiz

Section titled “Celadon Department Store roof: the prize quiz”

Saffron City Side quest

Parts of this entry open later: the Choice items need the Plain Badge, and Sabrina’s rematch needs your first Hall of Fame.

  • Choice items (after the Plain Badge): a merchant just north of the Route 6 gate (Saffron’s south exit) sells the Choice Scarf, Choice Specs and Choice Band for $50,000 each. Before the Plain Badge he has no stock.
  • Mr. Psychic’s house (south-east Saffron, west of the Route 8 gate): the psychic inside makes a Pokémon remember a move for one Heart Scale each time. For a repeatable supply, trade Big Pearls in Lavender Town.
  • Saffron Gym during the takeover: a Trainer sheltering just inside the Gym entrance offers a practice battle, repeatable.
  • Sabrina’s rematch (after your first Hall of Fame): she offers a battle, singles or doubles, Lv. 89–90, repeatable. It opens after your first Hall of Fame entry (the same unlock as Blaine’s rematch). After your final Hall of Fame entry (the one after you beat Giovanni at League HQ) she’s away from the Gym on Fridays (by the DS clock).
