How to read these tables

Morning is 4:00–9:59, day 10:00–19:59 and night 20:00–3:59 on the game clock; a table marked "any time" is the same all day. Swarms, Hoenn/Sinnoh Sound encounters, and special night-fishing replacements are not active in this hack and are excluded. "—" means the table stores no level or chance for that entry. The encounter rate is how often you meet wild Pokémon on that map (higher means more often).

These are the ordinary encounter tables. Calendar encounters can replace specific land slots. Visible spawns and capture-chain bonuses use additional rules; table weights are not shiny odds.

Gifts, trades and scripted encounters

  • Scripted wild battle: Bronzor (Lv. 25) — Ruins of Alph Underground Hall

    Give the injured girl outside the Ruins of Alph a Potion, then touch the fourth statue from the left in the Underground Hall’s top row. One chance: catching it, knocking it out or fleeing removes it; only losing allows a retry.

    See Ruins of Alph: the girl who saw her own face (→ Rock Incense)
  • Scripted wild battle: Entei (Lv. 90) — Ruins of Alph — Entei’s dream room

    After the final Hall of Fame and Lance’s visit to your house in Pallet Town. See Prof. Hale and Molly release the beasts in the Burned Tower before rematching the Mt. Mortar expedition leader; winning that rematch first can permanently block them. Return through the Kabuto chamber’s back wall and the statue hall to the dream room. One chance: catching it, knocking it out or fleeing removes it; only losing allows a retry.

    See Ruins of Alph: small extras, Ruins of Alph: the four stone panels and the chamber walls (continues Cherrygrove City to Azalea Town's Ruins of Alph entries)

Some of these depend on your starter, story progress or a choice. See the quests above.
