S.S. Anne

Other places

Quests and events

  • S.S. Anne: Teddiursa's honey and the salt (starts the party)
  • S.S. Anne: the party battles and the missing Teddiursa
  • S.S. Anne: defuse the bomb or the ship sinks (one chance)

Gifts, trades and scripted encounters

  • Trade: Butterfree for Raticate ("Raticate")

    Defeat the Raticate Gentleman on the S.S. Anne, then trade him a Butterfree during the ship visit.

  • Trade: Raticate for Butterfree ("Butterfree")

    First trade Butterfree for the Gentleman’s Raticate on the S.S. Anne; then trade a Raticate back in the 1F northwest rooms.

Some of these depend on your starter, story progress or a choice. See the quests above.
