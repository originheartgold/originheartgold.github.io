Where to find it

  • Gift (Lv. 70): Silph Co., staff cafeteria (hostages’ room).

    During the Saffron Rocket takeover, speak to the cafeteria hostage after Jessie, James and Maxie. Missable: collect Lapras before the Rocket Boss battle; the hostages leave afterward, win or lose. Leave a free party slot. Quest guide.

Chance per encounter, under the conditions shown.

  • Day: 10:00–19:59
  • Night: 20:00–3:59
